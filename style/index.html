Frequently asked questions

What icon styles does IconStash index?

IconStash classifies icons into 7 styles: Outline, Solid, Duotone, Fill, Bold, Thin, Light. Each style has a hub page listing its icons across all 28 indexed libraries, so you can hold the visual weight constant and compare how different design teams handle it.

What is the difference between outline and solid icons?

Outline icons are drawn as open strokes with no fill and read as light and technical; solid icons are fully filled and carry much more visual weight. Most products use outline for default state and solid for active or selected state, which is why libraries that ship both — such as Heroicons and Ionicons — are convenient.

Which icon style should I use?

Match the weight to your layout. Outline and thin suit dense interfaces and dashboards; solid and bold suit marketing pages, empty states, and large display sizes; duotone adds depth for feature cards and onboarding. The safest pattern is one outline set for structure plus its solid counterpart for active state.
