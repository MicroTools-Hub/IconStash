Design & UX integration

Aesthetic characteristics

Solar renders Notes Outline across six styles — Linear, Outline, Bold, Duotone, Broken and Bold Duotone. Broken and Duotone are the distinctive ones: they add a deliberate gap or a second colour layer, which is what makes the set popular for SaaS dashboards and mobile apps that need hierarchy without extra colour.

UX best practices

In file explorers, document lists and CMS dashboards, put the Notes Outline icon immediately to the left of the filename so the eye scans one straight line. If it triggers a destructive action, keep a confirmation step — a delete or archive glyph is too easy to hit by accident in a dense list.
