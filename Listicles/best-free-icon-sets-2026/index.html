37 Best Free Icon Sets for Developers in 2026

Every set ranked by design quality, license safety, maintenance cadence, and developer experience. All 37 are 100% free for commercial use — zero attribution, zero cost.

July 24, 2026 • 18 min read • By IconStash Team

How We Ranked These Icon Sets

We evaluated 60+ icon libraries and narrowed to 37 based on five criteria:

  • License: Must be MIT, Apache 2.0, ISC, or CC0. No "free with attribution" or proprietary EULAs.
  • Maintenance: At least one release in the past 6 months. Abandoned projects excluded.
  • Design consistency: Icons must share a unified grid, stroke weight, and visual language.
  • Developer experience: npm packages, tree-shaking, framework support (React/Vue/Svelte).
  • Icon count: Minimum 100 production-ready icons.

All 37 sets below are browsable on IconStash — search any concept and see results from every library side by side.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free icon set for developers in 2026?

Lucide Icons is the best overall: 1,979 icons, MIT license, weekly releases, native tree-shaking, and packages for every major framework. For maximum variety, Tabler (5,800+) and Phosphor (9,000+ with 6 weights) are excellent alternatives.

Are all these icon sets really free for commercial use?

Yes. All 37 sets use permissive licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, ISC, or CC0). You can use them in commercial products, SaaS, client work, and proprietary software without attribution or payment.

Which icon set has the most icons?

Material Symbols leads with 12,000+ (variable font). Among discrete SVG sets, Phosphor has 9,000+ (1,500 designs × 6 weights), followed by Tabler with 5,800+ unique outline icons.
