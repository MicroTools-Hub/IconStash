About fill icons

What this style means

Fill icons are filled icon variants — the same concepts as outline sets, rendered as solid shapes. IconStash classifies icons by style across all 28 indexed libraries, so you can hold the weight constant and compare how different design teams solve the same icon.

When to use them. Fill variants are the usual way to express state: outline for available, filled for active or selected. Because they mirror their outline counterparts, switching between them does not shift your layout.

Every icon below links to a page with the full SVG source, a live preview you can recolour and resize, PNG export at six sizes, and a ready-to-paste React snippet.
